Meet Dr. Nelson

More time to listen. More room to care.

I'm Renee Nelson, MD — a board-certified OB/GYN who spent 25 years caring for women as a generalist OB/GYN. I'm also a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner and a Women's Hormone Therapy Specialist.

I started my hotflash MD because the traditional medical system simply doesn't leave enough room for excellent menopause care.

Book a Consultation

Visits are too short. Follow-up is difficult. And too many women leave feeling unheard — or worse, told their symptoms are "just part of getting older."

By focusing solely on perimenopause and menopause through telehealth, I can give you the time, attention, and thoughtful follow-through you deserve. Honestly, that shouldn't feel revolutionary — but it does.

Healthcare should be a partnership, not a prescription. You deserve to be truly heard, thoughtfully cared for, and given the time that meaningful care actually requires. The best version of you isn't behind you — she's still ahead.

That's the practice I'm building: evidence-based, deeply personal, and grounded in partnership, because "good enough" should never be the ceiling for your health.

I look forward to partnering with you for your care.
