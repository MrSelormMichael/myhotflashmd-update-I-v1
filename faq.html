FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit. Don't see your question? Reach out anytime.

How does telehealth menopause care work?

You book a visit online, meet with your provider over secure video, and discuss your symptoms and health history. From there, we build a personalized treatment plan together. If medication is appropriate, prescriptions can be sent directly to your pharmacy.

Your first visit is a full 60 minutes — enough time to review your history, talk through your options, and start a plan that fits your life.

Is hormone replacement therapy (HRT) safe?

For many women, hormone therapy is a safe and effective option when guided by current clinical evidence and tailored to individual health history. Your provider will walk through your personal risks and benefits during your visit so you can make an informed decision.

HRT isn't right for everyone, and non-hormonal options are always available too.

Do you accept insurance?

No. The practice does not bill or accept insurance. A superbill can be provided for you to submit for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Labs and pharmacy prescriptions may still go through your insurance, depending on your coverage. Labs can also be completed at discounted cash-pay rates if you prefer.

What states do you see patients in?

Care is available only to patients who are located in California at the time of their visit.

If you're outside California, unfortunately we cannot see you — but we'd encourage you to find a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner near you.

Can you see Medicare patients?

No. I have not opted out of Medicare, so federal law does not allow me to see patients enrolled in Medicare or Medicare Advantage. Unfortunately, no exceptions can be made.

How long is a typical visit?

Initial visits are a full 60 minutes to allow time for a complete history and discussion of your goals. Follow-up visits are typically shorter and focused on how your plan is working — what's improving, what needs adjusting, and how you're feeling overall.

Will I need lab work?

Sometimes. Depending on your symptoms and history, your provider may recommend labs, which can often be completed at a local lab near you and reviewed together at a follow-up visit.

Labs can be billed through insurance, or you can use discounted cash-pay options.

Do you prescribe testosterone?

Yes. Testosterone can be considered when it is medically appropriate and fits your individual symptoms, health history, and goals. It's not right for everyone, and your provider will discuss whether it's a good option for you.

Do you prescribe GLP-1 medications for weight management?

Yes. FDA-approved GLP-1 medications may be part of a thoughtful weight and metabolic-health plan when medically appropriate. This is always individualized after a full medical review, and not every patient will be a candidate.

Do I need a membership?

Your initial 60-minute consultation is a stand-alone visit — you're not committing to anything ongoing.

If you choose to continue hormone therapy or weight-management care, an ongoing membership is required. This isn't a sales tactic — it's because finding the right combination of treatment takes time. Doses need adjusting, side effects need monitoring, and your needs can change over time. The membership model is what allows me to follow you closely and get your treatment right.

Is this practice for urgent or emergency care?

No. my hotflash MD does not provide urgent or emergency medical care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations or rescheduling with less than 48 hours' notice incur a $200 fee. No-shows are charged the full visit price.

How do I get started?

Simply book a visit using the link on this site. You'll receive instructions on how to prepare and what to expect before your appointment.

If you're not sure whether we're the right fit, feel free to reach out through the contact page first.

Still have questions?

Reach out directly or book a visit to talk it through with your provider.
