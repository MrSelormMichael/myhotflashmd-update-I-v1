Services

Menopause care, tailored to you.

From hormone therapy to symptom management, every service is delivered over secure telehealth visits with a plan built around your body.

Core Service

Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT)

Personalized, guideline-informed hormone therapy to manage hot flashes, night sweats, mood changes, sleep disruption, and more — with risks and benefits explained clearly so you can decide what's right for you.

  • Comprehensive symptom and health history review
  • FDA-approved estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone options
  • Lab work coordination where appropriate
  • Ongoing dose adjustments and follow-up

Symptom Management

Hot Flashes & Night Sweats

Whether or not hormone therapy is right for you, we offer a full range of options — hormonal and non-hormonal — to reduce the frequency and severity of vasomotor symptoms.

  • Non-hormonal prescription options
  • FDA-approved KNDy receptor antagonists
  • Lifestyle and trigger-management strategies
  • Personalized symptom tracking

Whole-Person Care

Mood, Sleep & Energy

Menopause affects more than hot flashes. We address the sleep disruption, mood changes, brain fog, and low energy that often come with this transition.

  • Sleep quality assessment and support
  • Mood and anxiety screening
  • Brain fog and cognitive symptom strategies
  • Referrals to specialists when needed

Long-Term Health

Bone & Heart Health Guidance

Menopause changes your risk profile for osteoporosis and cardiovascular disease. We help you understand those risks and build a proactive plan.

  • Risk assessment and screening recommendations
  • Nutrition and lifestyle guidance
  • Coordination with your primary care team

Weight & Metabolic Health

Weight Management & Metabolic Health

Hormonal shifts in menopause can slow metabolism and change where and how the body stores weight. We offer personalized, evidence-based support to help you manage weight and metabolic health during this transition.

  • Metabolic health assessment and lab review
  • Personalized nutrition and lifestyle strategies
  • FDA-approved GLP-1 medications when medically appropriate
  • Ongoing monitoring and plan adjustments

Sexual & Vaginal Health

Sexual & Vaginal Health

Straightforward, shame-free care for vaginal dryness, painful sex, low desire, and genitourinary symptoms. These are common, treatable, and worth talking about.

  • Vaginal estrogen and other local therapies
  • Non-hormonal moisturizers and lubricants
  • Testosterone when medically appropriate
  • Urinary symptom management

Labs & Prescriptions

Labs can be billed through insurance, or you can use discounted cash-pay options. Prescriptions can be filled at your preferred pharmacy using insurance when covered.

Insurance & Superbills

The practice does not bill or accept insurance. A superbill can be provided for you to submit for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Who We Can See

Care is available only to patients located in California at the time of their visit. We are unable to accept Medicare patients.

Visit Changes

Cancellations or rescheduling with less than 48 hours' notice incur a $200 fee. No-shows are charged the full visit price.
